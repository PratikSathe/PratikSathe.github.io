I am a researcher at [D-Wave Quantum](https://www.dwavequantum.com/), where I use quantum annealing devices to investigate problems in condensed matter and statistical physics. My work focuses on leveraging programmable quantum hardware to explore collective phenomena in complex many-body systems. Prior to joining D-Wave, I was an Information Science and Technology Institute ([ISTI](https://www.lanl.gov/engage/collaboration/nsec/information-science-and-technology-institute)) postdoctoral fellow at Los Alamos National Laboratory in the [Theoretical division](https://www.lanl.gov/engage/organizations/aldsct/theoretical) (T-4). I graduated with a PhD in Physics from UCLA in 2023, after receiving my bachelor's and master's degrees in engineering physics from IIT-Bombay.

Outside of work, I often click photos of birds and nature. For the latest pics, do visit my Instagram [page](https://www.instagram.com/birds.are.lit/).
